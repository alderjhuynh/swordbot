## Notes

- **Dead code** (implemented, never wired into the brain): none — `Uppercut`, `FallingSprintHit`, `Sweep.back`, `Crit.punish` are all now reachable. (`Crit.punish` remains only as a primitive for future use.)
- **Missing entirely**: nothing from the table above — reverse deflection (§6.1), peak crit, turn-back, double jump, whiff-punish, and TPS-aware decisions are now implemented.
- **Attack execution caveat**: `AttackOnce` attacks whatever `client.hitResult` is under the crosshair, not the locked target directly — if the `SmoothLook` wobble/error leaves the crosshair off the target at fire time, the bot can break a block (`startDestroyBlock`), whiff, or hit a different entity. This remains open.
